# Changelog

## 1.1.1
- Name: now simply "All in One QoL" (by Team Pscht).
- Cultivator: thistles can be planted too (5 thistles), bushes grow back after harvesting. Fix: bushes/mushrooms no longer disappear from the cultivator when another mod (e.g. More Vanilla Build Prefabs) disables them. Harvested bushes, mushrooms and thistles stay (mushrooms/thistles as a small dark stump) and show when they regrow. Regrow time adjustable in the config (default 300 minutes). Plant growth time is now set in minutes with a real default (67 minutes) instead of seconds and 0 (old values are converted).
- Cultivator grid: visible grid lines on the ground (green = will be planted, red = not enough seeds, grey = spot taken or ground not cultivated), seed counter on screen (e.g. "Carrot seeds: 7 / 9"), plants you lack seeds for glow red. Grid size also with Ctrl + mouse wheel (width) and Alt + mouse wheel (length), the mouse wheel alone rotates the whole grid. The grid no longer flips sides while you walk.
- Extra slots: new key slot below the trinket (swamp crypt key and every other door key, also from mods). Picked up keys move in by themselves, doors open as usual.
- Extra slots: after death, food, second hotbar, armor, shield, trinket and key go back into their slots when you loot your tombstone. The second hotbar on screen looks like the normal one again (was too opaque), opacity adjustable.
- Player: bigger inventory, up to 5 extra rows (rows bought from the trader come on top, max 9).

## 1.1.0
- New icon (Team Pscht, Deep North style).
- README: Team Pscht branding, links to other Team Pscht mods, support section with bug report and idea forms on GitHub.

## 1.0.0
- First public release. All-in-one quality of life for Valheim, every feature can be switched on/off in the config (sections 00–22), with server sync.
- Taming & breeding, planting in every biome, cultivator grid, bushes/mushrooms with the cultivator.
- Craft from containers, fill stations from chests, smart chests, chest labels/signs/icons, quick stack, sort.
- Portal names, targets and default portal; day/night length and clock; timers; radar.
- Extra slots (armor, utility, belt, trinket, shield, food) and a second hotbar (Alt+1 … Alt+8).
- Craft multiple items at once, favorites refilled from nearby chests, tooltip comparison, colored durability bars.
- Own skills: Exploring (map reveal radius), Sailing (ship speed), Taming (stronger tames).
- English and German: follows Valheim's language automatically, or choose it in the config (section 00).
