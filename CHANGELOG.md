# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First public release. All-in-one quality of life for Valheim, every feature can be switched on/off in the config (sections 00–22), with server sync.
- Taming & breeding, planting in every biome, cultivator grid, bushes/mushrooms with the cultivator.
- Craft from containers, fill stations from chests, smart chests, chest labels/signs/icons, quick stack, sort.
- Portal names, targets and default portal; day/night length and clock; timers; radar.
- Extra slots (armor, utility, belt, trinket, shield, food) and a second hotbar (Alt+1 … Alt+8).
- Craft multiple items at once, favorites refilled from nearby chests, tooltip comparison, colored durability bars.
- Own skills: Exploring (map reveal radius), Sailing (ship speed), Taming (stronger tames).
- English and German: follows Valheim's language automatically, or choose it in the config (section 00).
